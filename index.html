What you can study

Every group is built around a shared goal, so the lessons stay useful for everyone in the room.

Russian for foreigners

Start with the alphabet or continue from where you are now. You practise speaking, listening, reading and writing at your own level.

Russian for native speakers

You speak Russian at home but reading, spelling and grammar feel harder. We work on literacy and confident written Russian.

Pancyprian exam preparation

Focused preparation for the Russian exam in the Παγκύπριες Εξετάσεις, planned around your exam date.

Personal life and work

Russian for family, friends, travel and daily life, or for clients, colleagues and business correspondence.
